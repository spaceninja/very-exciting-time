## David Grusch Interview

- UAP whistleblower David Grusch: “ So in terms of what my exposure was, I had folks from the retrieval program, I'll call it exfiltrate audiovisual evidence for me to see, both video and photos. And that was… audiovisual evidence of the retrieved craft [and] some of the dead cadavers that were recovered… the pilots… And of course, I interviewed a ton of subjects all the way up to the cabinet White House level that had close personal participation in this subject…  I brought a lot of those people to the intelligence community inspector general… This complaint was… considered credible and urgent. [Director of National Intelligence] Avril Haines was forced to send it to the Senate and House intelligence committees in summer 2022, and then I subsequently provided 11 and a half hours of classified testimony to the Senate and House intelligence committees.”
- Grusch: “ Unfortunately, from what I understand, there was a little bit of fear in those committees, especially in the Senate, and they didn't open up a full field investigation. I think Mark Warner and Marco Rubio were a little nervous to really up the ante… And then, of course, the ICIG investigated it. And from what I understand, back-channelled to me unofficially is, Jake Sullivan in White House General Counsel Office basically told Tom Monheim, who was the Inspector General for the intelligence Community under Biden, and his staff to stop, and they realized they were bumping into White House classified programs, something called special access programs, and eventually they were told to stop, from what I understand… I hope Secretary Rubio is helping Stephen Miller at the White House, because I'm hearing mixed messages about Stephen Miller's seriousness. And I hope he understands how this will impact President Trump's legacy if this is botched.”
- Grusch: “ It's funny, I got tipped off initially, I got access, I have to be careful because some of this is still classified, to a peer adversary's intelligence reporting about what they thought was going on in the US. And I learned more from a particular peer adversary about what the US was doing in terms of retrieval and reverse engineering of UAP than I learned from my own government officially, until I started spelunking in different archives, talking to people that were able to provide me program documentation, et cetera. And then I had an individual that cleared me into a program that I could learn some of the signatures that we've learned that these craft emit, and how the US tracks it, and how they hide the tracking of UAP within the national intelligence architecture in plain sight… And… that foreign intelligence reporting, the Defense Intelligence Agency has refused to release those classified documents to Congress. And I know Representative Burlison is likely gonna pulse DIA leadership to get that.”
-  Dr Phil: “Now you say you have seen and have evidence that there are craft that didn't originate on this planet. Are we talking multiple craft?”
  - Grusch: “Yeah.”
  - Dr. Phil: “Are we talking five, six, 25, 50? What are we talking about?”
  - Grusch: “Under 100, but more than 25 is what I could say. And the exact numbers actually are a little nebulous, because starting in the early 2000s when a lot of money was siphoned for the global war on terror, the big defense contractors started doing their own retrievals sort of off books, under the cognizance of certain government agencies. So there's a lot in… private possession in defense contractor space that I think the government doesn't have a full accounting on. So it's really hard to get exact numbers, but certainly we've recovered several dozen vehicles over the last eight, nine decades or so.”
- Dr Phil: “ Do you know where some of these craft, if we have more than 25 and less than 100, do you know where some of these are being stored or researched as we sit here today?”
  - Grusch: “ Yeah, the problem with that is I knew where they were historically up until the date and time that my source at that facility worked at it. And this is something I told an element of Stephen Miller's task force recently is, they're gonna have to get eyes onto those facilities. There is a program protection strategy, unfortunately, to move moveable archives and materials if they feel like the location has been burned or exposed… Up until a couple years ago, I was aware of US government facilities and contractor facilities that both had the documentation and the material holdings and the cadavers…  But… certain things are hard to move. When they're a particular size, it's a little bit laborious to move. So I think there's some locations that likely are untouched because... I'll be very vague on that. Not to spook the herd.”
- Dr. Phil: “ And is there a homogeneity among these that suggests that they're all coming from the same place? Are they similar, different?”
  - Grusch: “There's a lot of vehicle types. You have the disc, you have the sunfish, boot-heel looking thing. You have boomerangs, you have crescent moon looking craft. You have Tic Tacs. You have egg looking craft as well. Certainly, it's very hard to deduce origin, because it's not like we know exactly what point in space or time these things came from.”
  - Grusch: “Different types of pilots, different types of craft, some similarities in operation and technology. It's sort of like if you're a car manufacturer, a lot of people build sports cars that are in the shape of a Porsche 911 or a Ford Mustang, but there's many different drivers of said sports cars, if you get the coarse analogy. So it's a whole taxonomy of different intelligences from very familiar type of intelligence, where they seem just a little more advanced than us, to indescribable, noncorporeal type of sentience.”
  - Grusch: “ You know, it goes back to the orb phenomenon that came out in tranche four, that the administration is having this issue with these luminous orbs showing up at a certain sensitive test range. What is that? You could see that it's intelligently controlled. They come in groups. This isn't just ball lightning here, but it's this plasmoid type intelligence. Now, there might be a structured craft behind said plasmoid, and that plasma discharge might be an artifact of the propulsion system possibly. But we're seeing even weird stuff like that, that is almost unknowable what it is, unless we have some way of studying it or capturing it that I don't know about.”
- Dr Phil: “ And you have also seen pictures, or video, or both, of life forms that are piloting these craft as well. You've seen the cadavers.”
  - Grusch: “ Yeah. Some of the craft we've assessed are likely drones, and some of the craft that we've recovered… were empty and the pilots were not there… But yes, some of it was piloted. And… what do you know? They look just like what people have reported seeing… What we would call in pop culture speak, grays. Small gray aliens, exactly the way you think… Some of them look exactly as reported by credible observers that have nothing to gain and everything to lose by discussing their experience.”
- Dr Phil: “ Have any of these pilots or occupants of these craft, to your knowledge, been taken alive?”
  - Grusch: Yes. And I know people who have had direct interactions in a detainment situation, which sounds very Star Trek and sounds very crazy, but it is true. I talked to… a former cabinet member about that, who directly worked the issue for a particular former vice president. You can guess which one. Darth Vader.”
- Dr Phil: “ And were they able to communicate?”
  - Grusch: “ Yes. And it is true that some sort of a consciousness communication… People call it telepathy. I cannot begin to tell you how it works, but there is some sort of communication like that. And even friends of mine that were on retrievals, felt that kind of communication as they approached the vehicle when they were… cordoning it off, waiting for transport. How that works, what that means, I certainly don't know.”
- Dr Phil: “ I'm learning about this as I go along, and I'm told that our nearest neighboring solar system is Andromeda, and that it would take two and a half million years traveling at the speed of light to get there. So if these visitors have traveled from the nearest solar system, it would take two and a half million years at the speed of light, which we can't do.”
  - Grusch: “Yes, the closest galaxy, I think it's M31, Andromeda, like you said, however, within our own galaxy, there's extra solar planets around other nearby stars.  I do caution to ascribe this whole phenomenon as merely just extraterrestrial. That's certainly a piece of the pie. That's likely what some of this is. But there's other taxonomies of anomalous intelligences… Perhaps it's ultra-terrestrial. Perhaps it grew up here, and it left, and it came back. And there's shadow biome. There's other types of origins. There's under the sea… We don't understand 70% of our oceans, and what advanced intelligence might have developed on the seabed?”
