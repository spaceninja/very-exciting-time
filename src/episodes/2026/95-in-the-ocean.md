## Matthew Brown on NHI Agreements

- UFO whistleblower Matthew Brown: "I have learned that it is the oral tradition in the legacy program itself that agreements were struck between the United States and a third party [NHI] in possession of this technology.”
- Brown: “That agreement in broad terms has two kinds of arrangements. One was that there are certain areas that the United States, within its borders, needs to stay away from. That is essentially someone else's territory. So no developments there, no military operations there, no commercial or human activity. That's one area of the agreement. And then the other side is some sort of access to and communication with humans of their choice. And that is the nature of at least one agreement, very old agreements sometime in the Truman and Eisenhower era that was arrived at. I'm sure that there are things in the lore that give more detail. I can't speak to the validity of those things, but I'm just sharing that now that that is in the legacy program itself, at least a line of... narrative"
- Jesse Michels: “In the lore, you have this idea of the Greada treaty, where Eisenhower, under the guise of going to the dentist in Palm Springs in 1954, instead jets off to Edwards Air Force Base, where some diplomatic relations are engaged with these beings. The common story is that the beings are given access to humans for medical experimentation, to American citizens… You think there’s an oral tradition in the legacy program where this is undocumented, but it’s sort of well known… that some sort of treaties were engaged in?”
  - Brown: “‘Agreements’ is the term that was told to me. That might be because treaties have to be ratified by Congress, but a business agreement does not. I don’t know anything about this Greada treaty. When there’s that level of detail, I’m skeptical, especially given the amount of time that has passed… If all this is real—and I have direct knowledge that aspects of it are concretely real—then our security apparatus would inject disinformation when there’s leaks or to preempt any sort of revelation that would be counter to our interests. So I think there’s a lot of half-truths or fabrications out there that have become unquestioned parts of the UFO narrative, but I think there’s a core truth there, and to me it makes sense that there has to be some sort of agreement between us and other nations regarding this topic, and between us a third party, otherwise our strategic behavior does not make sense.”
- Michels: “It’s almost like the UFO legacy program has this hermetic unspoken oral tradition, where things aren’t written down. Is that what you’re implying?”
  - Brown: “Yes. I’ve developed sources since then, and been approached by people… I think there is roughly a split between those who want reform and some degree of this to come out, and those who… very very much do not want any of it to come out. Dylan Borland [spoke about this in his interview] with you, where he said there was a decision made to stop briefing new people in the legacy program on the history of the program, so that you’re getting into the next generation of people that have no background into the ground truth of where this came from, what has happened, and why decisions were made. You’re starting fresh, and you get to avoid the complications of the past, probably except for a very small circle of senior leadership who would have the need to know. So that is kind of pointing towards, why is it an oral tradition? It’s because you have a bifurcation now of people who were briefed into legacy before that decision was made to stop telling people some version of the history of the program, and those who have since come into the program, did not receive that history, but people talk, and people have questions, especially with something as significant as this. Hence, the oral tradition.”
- Michels: “It’s so wild, you would think this was the most important stuff.”
  - Brown: “Which is why you would keep it secret, especially when it’s things like agreements that maybe the US was compelled to sign and probably may have had terms unfavorable to us. You certainly wouldn’t want something like that leaking, especially when you present yourself as the world’s hyperpower. And by the way, I’m not the only person out here talking about this. Neither is Dylan Borland. David Grusch under oath was asked about agreements and said that he could not provide further detail, but he hoped the people in the legacy program would come forward with that information.”
