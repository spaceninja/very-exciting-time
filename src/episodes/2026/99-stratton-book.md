He also discusses new eyewitness descriptions of encounters, and the paranormal activity affecting his family that seems to have followed him home from Skinwalker Ranch.

It’s gray and has a large head with small black eyes, no noticeable nose, and a small mouth. Startled, it turns to face me. No strange smells, no craft or electricity in the air, no strange feelings. I check the living room window, almost 9 feet off the ground. How could anyone or anything look at me from that window and disappear in seconds?”

## Black Triangles and Secret Programs

- “In 2005, I was promoted [to] Chief of Air and Space Warfare… One of my key responsibilities was to provide threat assessments for programs that were classified so deeply that very few people knew they existed… One of my teammates… Dr. James Lacatski… the Senior Intelligence Analyst for Missile Defense… showed me a video… taken recently by security forces near a nuclear weapons storage site. A black triangle aircraft with softly rounded corners… each point marked by a yellowish-white light. No wings. No tail. No exhaust. No visible propulsion of any kind.”
  - “But what froze me was the angle. The object had been captured mid-maneuver, banking so steeply it was nearly vertical. If any human-made aircraft attempted a turn so sharp for so long, it would fall out of the sky. This thing didn’t. The video showed it execute the maneuver smoothly, almost gracefully, as if conventional aerodynamic limits, the fundamental laws of physics, simply didn’t apply to it… Credible military eyewitnesses… said the craft accelerated from a standstill to several thousand miles per hour almost instantly… performing maneuvers that would tear apart any conventional aircraft.”
- “‘Please tell me it’s ours,’ [Jim] said. I shook my head. I didn’t recognize it at all… The flight performance capabilities were far more advanced than anything I knew we—or any other nation—would or could have fifty years in the future… I was determined to find out exactly what it was… I tapped into every connection I had… If this was a U.S. program, one of my trusted relationships had to know. But no one did. Not a single person I contacted could identify the aircraft. That left me with only three possibilities: I was being lied to, which I did not believe was the case; a foreign government had developed a beyond-next-generation technology that we were unaware of and penetrated some of our most sensitive and classified airspace with impunity, which I doubted; or we were looking at something truly anomalous—unexplainable and not made by humans.”
- “We… asked a few of the most senior analysts at DIA, old-timers who’d been in the building for years… There had been a lot of UAP records at DIA, they said, as unexplainable as the report and video we were focused on, but several years earlier, a mysterious group of men in suits had swept through the office with authority and taken everything: every historical UAP-related document, down to the last scrap of paper and the most obscure electronic file. It was as if a shadow had passed through the building, not just removing records but erasing any history of them. Our colleagues told us the effort wasn’t limited to hard copies; they went into the digital archives, purging decades of material from classified systems. Where a history of DIA’s involvement with UAP had once existed, there was now only a void.”
- “We learned about countless military personnel who had witnessed UAP in restricted airspace and reported them, only to be threatened into silence, told to sign NDAs… Some unknown program was hiding that information from people like me, whose job it was to be aware of all advanced air and space threats so we could prevent strategic surprise. I was the Chief of Air and Space Warfare at DIA! To say these circumstances frustrated me is an understatement.”
- “More than once, I was warned, sometimes politely, sometimes bluntly, that there was a deeply buried UAP program I wasn’t supposed to find, and that I should proceed cautiously… ‘What you’re looking for exists, but it’s behind doors they don’t even acknowledge exist, and they do not want that to change.’ Those conversations, from people who had no reason to impress me and every reason to stay silent, made it clear: This was real, long known, and deliberately hidden.”

## AAWSAP

- “In January 2008, multiple UAP sightings were reported… near Crawford, Texas, home to President George W. Bush’s ranch… Witnesses described large, silent aircraft with bright lights moving at high speeds, performing maneuvers far beyond the capabilities of any known aircraft. The story instantly made national headlines. Military radar tracks showed UAP, which were confirmed by FAA systems as well… That ranch wasn’t just a retreat; it was a diplomatic venue, a site of strategic importance. Any unexplained aerial activity in that vicinity had to be taken very seriously. U.S. Air Force fighter jets were scrambled.
  - “Jay, we did scramble on something, we did chase something. But we can’t tell you what we saw. The Air Force Office of Special Investigations [AFOSI] came in right after and had us sign nondisclosure agreements [NDAs]. We were ordered not to speak about it. But yes, it’s real, and yes, we scrambled for it.”
- “I made the decision right there in Texas. No matter how difficult it would be, come hell or high water, I would uncover the truth and flesh out every element of this hidden program. I would peel back every layer of deception and would not rest until whoever these people were and whatever they were doing was dragged into the light. But resolve alone wasn’t enough… We needed a real program with… funding… Fortunately, Senator Reid agreed to help us get what we needed… We called our program the Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP).”
- “By early 2009, the pressure was building. Our work inside AAWSAP had begun to attract attention from unknown elements. Certain channels were being closed, questions that once went unanswered were suddenly being met with hostility, and it was obvious that someone, somewhere, knew we were getting too close… Doors were starting to slam shut, but in the process, cracks were appearing too. For the first time, insiders within the hidden program were signaling that they wanted to talk.”

## Hitchhiker Effect

- “The activity at my house continued. Lights switched on and off by themselves. Footsteps paced up and down stairs and along the deck outside. Voices whispered from nowhere, sometimes clear enough to make out words, other times little more than muffled murmurs. Orbs would glide through walls and windows like they owned the place, sometimes pausing long enough to make their presence felt before drifting away.”
- “Despite seeing plenty of video evidence of UAP, whenever we tried to take photographs or video it always came out blank: video files with no image, photos that were nothing but solid black, or recordings that mysteriously corrupted the moment we attempted to review them. It became clear early on that whatever this was, it simply did not permit our investigations to document it. The equipment worked perfectly under normal circumstances, but the moment the phenomena was involved, it was as if the technology went blind.”
- “One night at 3:00 a.m., my youngest son, Jonny, who was thirteen at the time, was awakened by ‘balls of light’ swarming his room. He said he heard them “screaming,” which was a shock because… he was legally deaf… A variety of sizes from softballs to golf balls, some blue, some red, and the smallest ones white, and they swirled around him in the darkness. He said he got incredibly angry, and as his anger built, some of the blue lights started ‘vibrating,’ turning from blue to red and darting toward him, hitting him in the face, chest, and abdomen. He screamed, ‘Holy fuck!’ and threw his arms in the air, as if it would scare them away. Moments later, the lights left his room through the closed window facing the front of the house.”
- “[Michele saw] a bright light, like a spotlight, was casting enough illumination to reveal what looked like someone standing in the grass near the trees… turned to look directly at Michele over his right shoulder… It appeared to be a wolf about 7 feet tall, walking upright on its hind legs, and the ‘hand’ on the tree was a large paw with claws… It pulled its lips back, baring its teeth, and dug its claws into the bark of the tree… A small white orb… appeared in the trees behind the wolf. It continued staring at Michele while the light elongated and widened… a churning fog inside of it… The wolf… stepped into the light… [which] shrank and disappeared.”
- “A few days later, she came home only to be met at the door by our boys, who were frantic, upset, and holding their hunting rifles. They both witnessed what they described as a large wolflike creature in the backyard. They first saw it looking at them through the downstairs sliding glass door before it took off running on two legs. Frightened for their lives, they ran and grabbed their hunting rifles. By the time they returned, the creature was gone.”
- “After this, all of us would see portals open in the backyard at random times, but always in the same spot. They would always start as a smaller orb floating, then swell to at least 7 or 8 feet in diameter, never a perfect circle, but an elongated oval, edges rippling like heat over asphalt. I never saw anything step through, but more than once, Michele and Jonny swore they saw the same tall humanoid figures that appeared as dark defined silhouettes, watching them from inside the portals.”
